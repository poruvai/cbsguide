## Study Tips

### Effective Study Techniques

1. **Time Management**
   - Create a realistic study schedule
   - Allocate time based on subject difficulty
   - Include regular breaks

2. **Active Learning**
   - Make summary notes
   - Create mind maps for complex topics
   - Practice numerical problems regularly

3. **Revision Strategy**
   - Review regularly, not just before exams
   - Focus on NCERT textbooks
   - Solve previous year question papers

4. **Exam Preparation**
   - Understand the exam pattern
   - Practice time-bound mock tests
   - Focus on important topics with higher weightage

### Daily Study Routine

- **Morning**: Difficult subjects (Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry)
- **Afternoon**: Moderate difficulty (Biology, Accountancy)
- **Evening**: Theory subjects (History, Geography, Political Science)
- **Night**: Revision and practice questions

## Exam Preparation

### Before the Exam

- Complete syllabus at least 1 month before exams
- Revise important formulas and concepts daily
- Solve at least 5 sample papers per subject
- Practice writing answers within time limits

### During the Exam

- Read questions carefully
- Allocate time for each section
- Start with questions you're confident about
- Review answers if time permits
- Ensure neat handwriting and proper presentation

### Important Points

- NCERT textbooks are the primary source
- Board exam questions often come from NCERT examples
- Practice numerical problems from NCERT exercises
- Remember important dates, formulas, and definitions
