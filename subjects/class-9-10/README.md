# Class 9-10 Study Guide

Complete study guide for CBSE Classes 9 and 10 Board Examinations.

## Subjects Overview

### Mathematics
- **Number Systems**: Real numbers, rational and irrational numbers
- **Algebra**: Polynomials, linear equations, quadratic equations
- **Coordinate Geometry**: Cartesian plane, distance formula
- **Geometry**: Triangles, circles, constructions
- **Trigonometry**: Basic trigonometric ratios and identities
- **Mensuration**: Area and volume calculations
- **Statistics & Probability**: Data handling, mean, median, mode

### Science

#### Physics Topics
- Motion, force and laws of motion
- Gravitation
- Work, energy and power
- Sound
- Light - reflection and refraction
- Electricity
- Magnetic effects of electric current

#### Chemistry Topics
- Matter and its nature
- Atoms and molecules
- Chemical reactions and equations
- Acids, bases and salts
- Metals and non-metals
- Carbon compounds
- Periodic classification of elements

#### Biology Topics
- Cell structure and functions
- Tissues
- Life processes (nutrition, respiration, transportation, excretion)
- Control and coordination
- Reproduction
- Heredity and evolution
- Our environment

### Social Science

#### History
- The French Revolution
- Socialism in Europe and the Russian Revolution
- Nazism and the Rise of Hitler
- Forest Society and Colonialism
- Pastoralists in the Modern World
- Nationalism in India

#### Geography
- India - size and location
- Physical features of India
- Drainage system
- Climate
- Natural vegetation and wildlife
- Population
- Resources and development

#### Civics (Political Science)
- Democracy in the contemporary world
- Constitutional design
- Electoral politics
- Working of institutions
- Democratic rights

#### Economics
- The story of village Palampur
- People as resource
- Poverty as a challenge
- Food security in India
- Sectors of Indian economy
- Money and credit
- Globalization

### Languages

#### English
- Reading comprehension
- Grammar: Tenses, voice, narration, clauses
- Writing: Letter writing, article writing, story writing
- Literature: Prose, poetry, drama analysis

#### Hindi
- Reading comprehension (अपठित गद्यांश)
- Grammar (व्याकरण)
- Writing skills (लेखन कौशल)
- Literature (साहित्य)

## Important Tips

1. **Focus on NCERT**: Board exams are primarily based on NCERT textbooks
2. **Practice Numericals**: Especially in Mathematics and Science
3. **Make Notes**: Create concise notes for quick revision
4. **Previous Papers**: Solve at least last 5 years' question papers
5. **Time Management**: Practice completing papers within 3 hours

## Exam Pattern

- Duration: 3 hours
- Total Marks: Varies by subject (usually 80 marks theory + 20 marks internal)
- Question Types: Multiple choice, short answer, long answer
- Passing Marks: 33% in each subject

## Key Formulas to Remember

### Mathematics
- Area of circle = πr²
- Pythagoras theorem: a² + b² = c²
- Quadratic formula: x = [-b ± √(b²-4ac)] / 2a
- Distance formula: d = √[(x₂-x₁)² + (y₂-y₁)²]

### Physics
- Speed = Distance / Time
- Force = Mass × Acceleration
- Work = Force × Displacement
- Power = Work / Time
- Ohm's Law: V = IR

### Chemistry
- Mole concept: Number of moles = Given mass / Molar mass
- Avogadro's number: 6.022 × 10²³
